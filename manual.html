The Omega Reach
by shiftBacktick

Wake up

Reach for the stars, gather their instruments, and curate the best collection in the universe! The Omega Reach is a point-and-click museum simulator (and dungeon crawler) where you are a musical genius.

Accessibility

The Omega Reach was originally developed for Games for Blind Gamers 5. It is designed to conform with WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) 2.2 at Level AA. Its menus are fully operable with a keyboard and screen readers such as NVDA. It is recommended to turn off speech interrupts for typed characters for the best experience.

Controls

The Omega Reach supports a variety of input devices—each providing a unique way to interact. Although its menus can be navigated intuitively like a web page, the unique controls for each device type are detailed in the rest of this section.

Input preferences

You will be prompted during the tutorial to select your preferred input settings for the best experience. They can be changed at any time from the Input Settings screen. Here is an explanation of these settings:

  • Input preference. This determines where the solutions to interactions are placed. This is necessary because each device type has its strengths. For example, a keyboard may explore objects at fixed points around them, whereas a mouse may click anywhere on the front hemisphere. This also affects what buttons are shown in tutorials.
  • Hold interactions. This determines how long a solution must be held to register. When enabled, the correct solution (or button to skip) must be held for one second. Otherwise, interactions are instantaneous. You will be prompted for any interactions that may be destructive.

Gamepad controls

The Omega Reach is best experienced with a gamepad for its analog controls and haptic feedback. Use an Xbox-compatible controller for the best results. Others can be configured with utilities like x360ce.

The conservatory is navigated with the Directional Pad. Interact with objects by holding either Trigger or Bumper and then moving the corresponding Analog Stick. These interactions can also be skipped by pressing any Face Button.

Haptic feedback

Vibration can be reduced or removed from the Input Settings screen. Beware that vibration is helpful for successfully interacting with objects.

Keyboard controls

The Omega Reach can also be played entirely with a keyboard.

The conservatory can be navigated with the Arrow Keys. Interact with objects by pressing most keys, from 1 to /, to Z to =. These interactions can also be skipped by pressing Space or Enter.

Mouse controls

The Omega Reach can also be played entirely with a mouse.

The conservatory can be navigated by clicking the arrow buttons on the screen. Interact with objects by clicking anywhere else on the screen. These interactions can also be skipped by clicking the button to interact.

Hey! I like using the Mouse Wheel to zoom in and out. You can also hold Shift to scroll left and right.

Touch controls

The Omega Reach can also be played entirely with a touchscreen. It works like using a mouse.

MIDI controls

The Omega Reach can be partially played with a MIDI device.

Interact with objects by sending a note on event to any channel. An 88-key keyboard works great! Limited support for modulation wheel, pitch bend, and sustain pedal is also included.

You will need to supplement your MIDI device with another supported input device to navigate the conservatory and skip interactions.

How to play

The Omega Reach rewards exploration and experimentation. You will be prompted whether to enable its optional tutorial. Please try exploring at your own pace before referencing this section.

As the curator for The Omega Conservatory, you are entrusted with its eponymous reach: a telescopic hand that extends into the past. With it, you examine distant galaxies, stars, planets, and moons in search for instruments to add to your collection. Once recovered and fully appraised, you may play them freely in your gallery, or trade them for whatever the gift shop is offering.

The Omega Conservatory

The conservatory is laid out in a grid that can be naviagted with up, down, left, and right controls. It consists of five main rooms:

  • The atrium. At the center, the atrium connects all the other rooms.
  • The reach. To the north, the reach is how you'll gather new instruments.
  • The lobby. To the west, the lobby is where donations accumulate.
  • The gallery. To the south, the gallery holds your instruments.
  • The shop. To the east, the shop will offer new instruments to buy.

Typically, you may find yourself recovering new instruments with the reach, collecting donations from the lobby, buying any available instrument from the shop, and appriasing them in the gallery. This loop can be repeated until you are satisfied with your collection of instruments.

Secondary rooms

There are other detours of interest beyond the main loop They have unique interactions which are detailed in later sections:

  • The stockroom. North of the shop, it holds its valuable wares.
  • The cellar. South of the shop, it's a place of unspeakable evils.
  • The reliquary. South of the lobby, its unique relics transcend spacetime.
  • The emporium. North of the lobby, it's where cycles end and begin.

Interactions

Nearly all objects inside and outside of the conservatory are interactive. To interact with an object, you must find its sweet spot. Press the controls and let the audio, visual, or haptic cues guide you to the correct solution.

Visually, objects will grow brighter and shake when near the sweet spot. Additionally, a sound will grow louder and higher pitched, and vibrations will grow stronger, as you get closer.

If you are having difficulty with a certain interaction, then you may skip it without penalty. This can be done with a special button press.

The Omega Reach

The reach is your first interactive object—and test—in The Omega Reach. Once online, it zooms in progressively to the following levels:

  • The horizon. Interact to reach new galaxies.
  • Galaxies. Interact to reach new stars. You can scroll between the galaxies you've reached.
  • Stars. Interact to reveal its planets and quirks. You can scroll between the stars you've reached in this galaxy.
  • Planets. Interact to reveal its moons and quirks and recover instruments. You can scroll between the planets around this star.
  • Moons. Interact to reveal its quirks and recover instruments. You can scroll between moons around this planet.

After the tutorial, the reach may be powered off and on at any time. When offline, you are unable to reach into the horizon.

Your collection

The instruments that you collect will appear in the gallery. They appear randomly in rarities that range from common to legendary.

Interact with an instrument to reveal it to the public. At least one instrument must be revealed for the conservatory to remain open, otherwise it will close temporarily. This affects your ability to access services other than the reach.

Instruments may have additional quirks to appraise, which increase in likelihood with their rarity. Once an instrument is fully appraised, you may play with them freely.

The economy

Credits ¤ are the currency of The Omega Reach.

You will accumulate donations while you explore the universe and play your instruments. They can be collected by visiting the lobby at any time. You will receive the most credits from thoroughly examining everything that you find.

Beware! The conservatory must be open to accrue donations.

You will receive periodic offers from the shop to exchange credits for unique instruments. Their costs scale with your wallet. At first, they may be an interesting gamble, but they soon become your gold sink.

Be quick! The shopkeeper will take a lunch break whenever you chat. They're typically gone for about a minute in real-time.

To curate your collection, you may sell any instrument at any time. You will earn credits based on their rarity and number of quirks. Holographic instruments will sell for even more!

Importantly, instruments are sold from the gallery by interacting.

The stockroom

The stockroom hides the shopkeeper's best finds. It opens whenever they take a lunch break. Its wares refresh every break.

Reveal and fully appraise an item to enable the option to steal them. Interact to add and remove them from your inventory.

Beware! If the shopkeeper catches you exiting from the stockroom, or fainted inside the cellar, then you will lose all of the stolen items from your inventory.

If you enter the gallery, or catch the shopkeeper from another angle, then you'll keep everything you've stolen from the stockroom.

The cellar

Beware! This area is best discovered at your own pace.

The cellar is a maze of your creation. It opens whenever the shopkeeper takes a lunch break. Its layout resets with their every break.

Sanity ☥ is the main resource of the maze. You begin each visit with an amount of sanity points that correlates to the number of instruments in your gallery. Points are spent normally by moving into new tiles in the maze for the first time. You will be prevented from revealing new tiles when it would cause you to spend your last point.

Certain tiles in the maze have anomalies to examine. Anomalies may be purely benign, extremely rewarding, or risky enough to end your run prematurely. Approach them cautiously with low sanity.

You will faint whenever your sanity points are depleted. When you wake in the atrium, you may notice that some credits are missing.

Try checking the lobby after you faint. The shopkeeper is less greedy than you think!

Greed ⸸ is a special resource that can be purchased inside the maze. It is automatically spent to prevent you from fainting. Beware that greed is lost upon leaving the maze!

Unique tiles

The cellar has tiles which allow traversal between its floors:

  • The ascent. Interact to reach a higher floor.
  • The descent. Interact to reach a lower floor.
  • The nexus. Interact to teleport to important locations in the cellar.

It has tiles which appear once per floor:

  • The balance. Every visit. Inverts sanity around half of its maximum.
  • The contraption. Every visit. Sanity trends toward 1 or its maximum.
  • The fountain. Every visit. Restores one sanity. Interact to donate credits.
  • The henge. Every visit. Restores sanity to four when below four points.
  • The juncture. Every visit. Restores sanity by half of current sanity.
  • The median. Every visit. Sanity becomes half of its maximum.
  • The pit. Every visit. Drains one sanity. Interact to gain greed.
  • The rift. Every visit. Drains sanity by half of current sanity.
  • The ziggurat. Every visit. Sanity trends toward half of its maximum.

And tiles which only appear once per run:

  • The armory. Once per run. Interact to gain greed while supplies last.
  • The kiln. Once per run. Interact to forge an instrument with credits.
  • The obelisk. Once per run. Interact to increase maximum sanity.
  • The quagmire. Once per run. Garnishes donations. Interact to destroy.
  • The trove. Once per run. Interact to transfer credits from the lobby.
  • The vision. Once per run. Reduces maximum sanity. Interact to destroy.

Unique tiles are gradually introduced in a random order as you explore deeper into the cellar. Consider how they might turn sanity, greed, and credits into resouces to exploit. Might they extend your runs indefinitely?

The reliquary

Beware! The reliquary is only available in the full version of The Omega Reach. Here you will collect relics while exploring the universe. They can be freely played and inspected to reveal their unique lore.

The emporium

Beware! The emporium is only available in the full version of The Omega Reach. Here you will be able to auction off your collection to start anew, carrying over your relics and special bonuses.

System requirements

The Omega Reach is best experienced with headphones and a gamepad.

Real-time synthesis is an extremely CPU-intensive process. It will attempt to run on any machine that meets its minimum requirements; however, you may experience stuttering or drop-outs when running below the recommended requirements.

Minimum system requirements

CPU SSE2 capable (64-bit)
RAM 2 GB

Browser support

The HTML5 build is intended to be played in a recent version of a Chromium-based browser, such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Opera. Browser extensions that alter page behavior are not recommended. Other browsers must support modern JavaScript features and the current Web Audio API specification.
